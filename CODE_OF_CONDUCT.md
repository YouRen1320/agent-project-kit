# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors and maintainers must communicate respectfully, review ideas on their merits, and make the project welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Expected behavior includes:

- Giving actionable, evidence-based feedback.
- Respecting privacy, security boundaries, and responsible disclosure.
- Acknowledging mistakes and correcting them constructively.
- Keeping disagreements focused on project outcomes and tradeoffs.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, sustained disruption, or knowingly unsafe contributions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate this standard. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions.

Report conduct concerns privately at [lijunpg@foxmail.com](mailto:lijunpg@foxmail.com). Do not include confidential information in a public issue.

Maintainers should handle reports consistently, minimize unnecessary disclosure, and avoid conflicts of interest when deciding outcomes.
